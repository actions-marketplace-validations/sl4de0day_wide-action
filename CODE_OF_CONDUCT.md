# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers pledge to foster an open, respectful, and constructive engineering environment for all participants.

## Expected Behavior

- Keep technical feedback specific, reproducible, and respectful.
- Focus on security engineering quality and user experience.
- Respect responsible disclosure and maintainer review decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, or abusive conduct.
- Disclosing private credentials or intentionally submitting malicious action workflows.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions or restrict access for violations reported through project issue or advisory channels.
